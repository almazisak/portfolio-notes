Spent today drafting the first jobs (JTBD) the app needs to get done, and put together a list of questions I want to ask users – starting with my mother-in-law 🥲
- The last time you let someone take something on credit, how did you write it down and what happened next?
- Has anyone ever paid you back later or only partly? What did you do to get your money?
- Has a customer ever disagreed with how much they owed? How did that play out? 
- Who else besides you writes down the debts? How do you make sure nothing gets missed?
- When did you last go through and recount all the debts in your notebook? What made you do it?
