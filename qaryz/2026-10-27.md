My mother-in-law runs a small food business, and like many places here, customers often buy "on tab". She tracks it all by hand: notebooks full of names and amounts, crossed out and rewritten as people pay.

Every so often she'd sit down with her son, my brother-in-law and a backend developer, to move everything into big spreadsheets and send each customer what they owed. Eventually he got tired of it and decided to build her an app instead.

The code wasn't the hard part. UX was, and he kept coming to me for advice. The more I looked, the more interested I got, so I joined.

That's how Qaryz started: a simple debt ledger for small shops and cafés.
